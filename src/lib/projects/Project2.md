In this *absolutely real* project, I created a safari to observe backend framework animals in their natural habitat. Watch Django elephants stomp through databases and Flask foxes sneak around microservices.

## Features

- **Database Watering Hole**: Watch Django elephants drink from SQL pools.
- **Microservice Forest**: Spot Flask foxes darting between trees.
- **API Dam**: See Express.js beavers hard at work.

## Lessons Learned

I learned that backend animals are surprisingly territorial. The Django elephants kept chasing the Flask foxes away from their watering hole!
